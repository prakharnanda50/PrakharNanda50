## Background

Parking areas near the academic blocks fill before 9:30 AM, and the internal shuttle runs without a published schedule. Staff and students circle the campus looking for space.

## Proposal

Build a campus mobility system that helps students and staff find parking and track internal transportation.

## Desired capabilities

- real-time parking availability
- campus parking map
- shuttle tracking
- estimated shuttle arrival time
- congestion monitoring
- administrator dashboard

## Open questions

- Are sensors or manual counts used for occupancy?
- Does the shuttle vendor allow GPS units on the vehicles?
