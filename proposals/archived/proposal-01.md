## Background

Students often struggle to find study materials, to find seniors who can guide them, or to buy and sell used textbooks and lab equipment. Juniors currently depend on personal contacts, and material shared in messaging groups is lost each semester.

## Proposal

Build a localized web/mobile platform that connects juniors with senior mentors, provides academic resources, and enables a small campus marketplace for used academic goods.

## Desired capabilities

- student profiles
- branch/year filtering
- mentor discovery
- messaging between students and mentors
- resource upload and download
- marketplace for used academic goods (textbooks, lab equipment)
- mentor ratings and reviews

## Open questions

- Who moderates uploaded material?
- Is payment handled on-platform or in person?
