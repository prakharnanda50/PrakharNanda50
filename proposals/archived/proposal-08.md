## Background

Employers and universities abroad regularly ask the Registrar to verify certificates and transcripts, which takes days and is done by post or email.

## Proposal

Build a secure academic credential verification platform with tamper-evident records.

## Desired capabilities

- digital certificate issuance
- transcript issuance
- tamper-evident records
- blockchain / smart-contract integration
- QR or hash-based public verification
- credential revocation

## Open questions

- Which chain or ledger, if any?
- How are lost keys handled?
