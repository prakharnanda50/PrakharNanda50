## Background

Counselling appointments are booked weeks ahead during exams, and many students never approach the cell. A confidential first point of contact was requested in the 2022 feedback round.

## Proposal

Build a confidential student wellness platform. It must be positioned as a wellness-support tool and not as a medical diagnostic system.

## Desired capabilities

- stress self-assessment
- mood tracking
- journaling
- guided relaxation and meditation
- anonymous peer support
- redirection to counsellor resources
- supportive AI conversation

## Open questions

- How is anonymity preserved?
- What is the escalation path when a student appears to be at risk?
