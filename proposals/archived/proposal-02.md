## Background

The campus has no single view of how much electricity, water and paper it uses, or of the emissions caused by commuting. Meter readings are kept in separate registers by the Facilities office.

## Proposal

Build a platform that helps the college monitor and reduce its environmental footprint by tracking electricity, water, paper usage, commuting emissions and sustainability activities.

## Desired capabilities

- green points for sustainable actions
- department and hostel leaderboard
- carbon calculator
- sustainability notifications
- energy-saving recommendations

## Open questions

- Can smart meters be installed in more than two blocks?
- How are commuting emissions collected without intrusive surveys?
