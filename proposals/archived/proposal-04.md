## Background

Hostel residents report running short of money before month-end and disputes over shared mess and canteen bills. Existing payment apps are not designed for small, frequent student expenses.

## Proposal

Build a financial management application designed specifically for college students.

## Desired capabilities

- daily expense tracking
- hostel expense tracking
- mess/canteen bill splitting
- savings goals
- receipt splitting
- automated expense logging
- budget alerts

## Open questions

- Can expense logging be automated from SMS or UPI notifications?
- What data stays on-device?
