## Background

Instruments are booked through paper registers. Returns are inconsistently logged, faults are discovered by the next user, and technicians have no view of which equipment is in use.

## Proposal

Build a centralized laboratory equipment management platform, with low-cost sensors for equipment health monitoring where practical.

## Desired capabilities

- equipment booking
- availability tracking
- QR check-in and check-out
- maintenance logging
- equipment status display
- late-return penalties
- technician dashboard
