## Background

During the 2022 fire drill the security desk could not locate callers, and the health centre learned of an injury by word of mouth. Mobile networks are congested in emergencies.

## Proposal

Build a campus emergency coordination platform for students, staff, security and medical teams.

## Desired capabilities

- one-tap SOS
- GPS and location sharing
- emergency alerts
- security-team notifications
- medical-team notifications
- GIS-based command dashboard
- resilient and offline communication concepts

## Open questions

- How does the app behave without mobile data?
- Who monitors the dashboard after hours?
