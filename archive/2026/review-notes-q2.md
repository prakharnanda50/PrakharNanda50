# Review notes - second quarter 2026

Ref: RN-2026-Q2 | Register entry: R-09

Notes restored from the panel convener's folder.

The panel met twice in April and once in May. Points agreed so far:

1. The review brief should describe what a finished tool must do, not
   how it is built.
2. Outputs expected: a generated term timetable, a list of unresolved
   conflicts, and a way for the Academic Office to adjust individual
   slots by hand.
3. Inputs expected: faculty availability and load, room inventory with
   capacities and equipment, section and batch lists, subject hours.
4. The data supplied in January (CAMPUS-09 series) is sufficient to
   begin. Faculty duty slots need confirming with HoDs.
5. The panel is open to an agent-based approach but does not want to
   prescribe one.
