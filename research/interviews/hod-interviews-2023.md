# Interviews with Heads of Department, 2023

Ref: IV-2023-01

**HoD CSE:** We build the timetable in a shared spreadsheet. If one faculty member changes, five other sections have to move. I spent two evenings last term just moving lab slots.

**HoD ECE:** Labs are the hard part. A microcontroller lab needs a room with the right benches, and only three rooms qualify. Those are also wanted by the EEE department.

**HoD EEE:** Faculty who have duty in the exam cell cannot teach at certain times, but the timetable builder does not know that. We find out when a class is missed.

**Academic Office:** The room chart and the department timetables are separate. We check conflicts by eye.
