# Archive guide

## Layout

* `archive/` - registers by year
* `research/` - surveys, observations, interviews, reports, field notes
* `proposals/archived/` - proposal documents; `draft-a`, `draft-b`, `draft-c` hold working papers
* `meeting-notes/` - committee minutes
* `datasets/` - data exports
* `diagrams/`, `media/` - figures and images
* `references/` - charter, numbering, registry export
* `.archive/` - manifest and access log (dot-prefixed folder)
* `.review/` - review queue listing (dot-prefixed folder)

## Status labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Under Discussion | Received and being considered |
| Archived | Closed; kept for the record |
| Deferred | Put off until a stated condition is met |
| Merged | Folded into another programme |
| Withdrawn | Pulled by the sponsor |
| Revisit | A previously archived record has been reopened; it appears in the review queue |

## Notes

Many older files were imported from departmental folders and use
different conventions. Where two documents disagree, the registry export
and the status logs are taken as authoritative.
