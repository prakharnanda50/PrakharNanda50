## Brief as submitted

### 4. Student Financial Wellness & Micro-Budgeting App

- **Theme:** FinTech
- **Difficulty:** Average
- **Description:** Managing pocket money and hostel expenses can be tough for engineering students. Design a specialized finance app that allows students to track daily expenses, split mess/canteen bills, and set savings goals.
- **Key Features:** SMS/notification parsing for automated expense logging (optional/privacy-focused), smart receipt splitting for group outings, and AI-driven budget alerts.
