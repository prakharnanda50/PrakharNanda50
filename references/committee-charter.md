# Campus Innovation Committee - Charter (extract)

Ref: CH-01 | Adopted 2022-03-09

1. The committee receives technology proposals from departments,
   cells and student bodies and records them in this archive.
2. Each proposal is given an archive record number (AR-NN).
3. The committee meets at least twice a year. Minutes are numbered MN-NN.
4. A proposal may be Under Discussion, Archived, Deferred, Merged,
   Withdrawn or Revisit (see archive guide).
5. The secretariat maintains the archive and the registry export.
